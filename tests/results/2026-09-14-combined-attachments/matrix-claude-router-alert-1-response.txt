**3. Responsibilities**
Not atomic. Four responsibilities:
- R1: fire on a five-minute schedule.
- R2: read the supplied queue length and the configured threshold.
- R3: compare the two numbers and decide breach.
- R4: dispatch one alert through the existing notification service when R3 says breach.

**4. Routing decisions**
- R1: deterministic code. A timer needs no decision.
- R2: deterministic code. Reading two known values from known places is a lookup.
- R3: deterministic code. A numeric comparison is a fixed rule with no content interpretation.
- R4: deterministic code. The destination and message are fixed. The single-alert constraint is a state flag or idempotency key, not a judgement.
- Workflow axis: a fixed linear sequence R1 to R4 with no branching beyond one conditional. A plain scheduler is enough. No workflow engine is required.
