### Runtime and control placement

- **Hosting:** The mapping does not decide in-process versus separately hosted execution. If the peer is separately hosted, the hosting boundary introduces isolation, lifecycle, ingress, and identity concerns.
- **Ingress and binding:** A2A delegation is layered over the peerΓÇÖs ingress contract. Resolve the peerΓÇÖs accepted request/response contract and binding before attempting delegation.
- **Isolation and lifecycle:** If a hosted runtime is selected, do not assume interaction sessions outlive the hostΓÇÖs idle/reclaim policy. The surface reference records per-session sandbox isolation as observed on 2026-09-04/05, not as a permanent SLA.
- **Identity:** A caller identifier reaching the hosted agent does not itself authorize acting as that caller downstream. Delegated access requires an explicit consent mechanism where applicable.
- **Human authority:** A handoff workflow gate is a workflow-level authority boundary. It is distinct from per-tool-call approval and from delegated-access consent.

### Assurance

Capture independent execution evidence for:

- session continuity across multiple turns;
- retrieved sources and their use in the response;
- handoff transitions, gate decisions, and outcomes;
- A2A agent-card discovery, negotiated binding, delegated task, and result.

Evaluate each responsibility against its own requirement, then verify that observed behavior still supports the routing decision. In particular, confirm that the implementation has not silently turned peer delegation into a typed tool call, or turned the optional workflow into an always-on orchestration. Re-run these checks after changes.
